About

The journey, not the trophy.

I'm Harshu — a student of design and code from India. I spend my days at the intersection of health, wealth and financial independence: learning how bodies work, how money compounds, and how products get built.

This portfolio frames the journey — not a finished career. Every project here is one season of it. Some shipped, some broke, all of them taught me something. I like quiet interfaces, loud ideas, and the patience of compounding.
